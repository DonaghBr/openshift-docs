= Understanding AI workload management options

[role="_abstract"]
Choose the right operator for your AI workload based on your scheduling, coordination, and scaling requirements. {product-title} provides three operators that work together to manage AI and machine learning (ML) workloads.

== Operator overview

{kueue-name}::
A Kubernetes-native queuing system that manages access to resources for jobs. {kueue-name} determines when a job waits, is admitted to start, or should be preempted. Use {kueue-name} to provide structured queues and prioritization so that workloads are handled fairly and efficiently.
+
{kueue-name} supports all-or-nothing semantics, heterogeneous and elastic resources, and integrates with the existing Kubernetes API server, scheduler, and cluster autoscaler.
+
For more information, see xref:../kueue/about-kueue.adoc#about-kueue[Introduction to {kueue-name}].

{lws-operator}::
Deploys groups of pods as a single, manageable unit for multi-node AI/ML inference. The {lws-operator} treats groups of pods as one unit to simplify scaling, recovery, and updates for large workloads such as sharded large language models (LLMs).
+
Each group consists of one leader pod and multiple worker pods, all managed together with identical lifecycle stages. Rollouts, rolling updates, and pod failure restarts are performed as a group.
+
For more information, see xref:../leader_worker_set/index.adoc#lws-about[{lws-operator} overview].

{js-operator} (Technology Preview)::
Manages large-scale, coordinated workloads such as high-performance computing (HPC) and AI training. The {js-operator} provides automatic failure recovery, stable networking, and startup control for multi-job workflows.
+
If one part of a large training job fails, the {js-operator} can restart the entire group of workers from a saved checkpoint. Stable headless services ensure workers can find and communicate with each other, even after a failure.
+
For more information, see xref:../jobset_operator/index.adoc#js-about[{js-operator} overview].

== When to use each operator

.Operator comparison
[cols="1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| Capability
| {kueue-name}
| {lws-operator}
| {js-operator}

| Primary use case
| Job queuing and quota management
| Multi-node inference (sharded LLMs)
| Distributed training and HPC

| Scheduling model
| Queue-based admission with quotas
| Group-level lifecycle management
| Coordinated multi-job execution

| Failure handling
| Preemption and re-queuing
| Group-level restart
| Checkpoint-based recovery

| Scaling
| Cluster-wide quota enforcement
| Group replica scaling
| Multi-template job support

| Integration
| Works with the {lws-operator}, {js-operator}, and other frameworks
| Requires {cert-manager-operator}
| Standalone or with {kueue-name}
|===

== Personas

Different personas interact with AI workload management:

Batch administrators:: Manage the cluster infrastructure and establish quotas and queues.
Batch users:: Run jobs on the cluster, such as researchers, AI/ML engineers, or data scientists.
Serving users:: Run inference workloads to expose trained AI/ML models.
Platform developers:: Integrate Operators with other software or contribute to upstream projects.
