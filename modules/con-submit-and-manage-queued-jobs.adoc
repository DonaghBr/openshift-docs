= Submitting and managing queued jobs

[role="_abstract"]
Submit Kubernetes jobs to {kueue-name} queues and manage their lifecycle. {kueue-name} manages `Workload` objects that represent the resource requirements of a job, controlling when jobs are admitted, suspended, or preempted based on configured quotas.

== How {kueue-name} manages jobs

{kueue-name} does not directly manipulate jobs. Instead, it automatically creates a `Workload` object for each job and syncs decisions and statuses between the two objects. When a job is submitted:

. The job is suspended by default.
. {kueue-name} creates a corresponding `Workload` object.
. When resources are available and quota permits, {kueue-name} admits the workload and resumes the job.
. The Kubernetes scheduler assigns pods to nodes.

== Submit jobs to a queue

To submit a job with quota management, you must identify the local queue in your namespace and add the appropriate queue label to your job specification.

For procedures, see xref:../kueue/running-kueue-jobs.adoc#running-kueue-jobs[Running jobs with quota limits].

== Manage workloads and label policies

Control how {kueue-name} discovers and manages jobs in your namespace:

* *Label namespaces* — Configure which namespaces {kueue-name} monitors for jobs.
* *Configure label policies* — Control whether {kueue-name} manages unlabeled jobs or only jobs with explicit queue labels.

For procedures, see xref:../kueue/managing-workloads.adoc#managing-workloads[Managing jobs and workloads].
